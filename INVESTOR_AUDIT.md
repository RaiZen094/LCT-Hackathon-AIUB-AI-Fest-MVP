# Investor-readiness audit

## Gaps found in the original MVP

- Strong product demonstration, but the buyer and economic value were implicit.
- No dedicated investor narrative or guided 3–5 minute pitch sequence.
- No explicit competitive thesis against manual work, chatbots, SaaS tools, or custom automation.
- No business-model, go-to-market, beachhead, or expansion-path framing.
- Prototype evidence was clear, but validation status and next proof points were not consolidated.
- Trust was demonstrated through human approval, but broader governance and privacy roadmap items were missing.
- No editable founder placeholders for market, traction, pilots, pricing, proof, ask, or milestones.
- CleanDhaka emphasized social impact more than operational and institutional value.

## Implemented

- Added Investor View and nine-step Pitch Mode.
- Added buyer, user, beneficiary, Bangladesh beachhead, and expansion framing.
- Added product-thesis flow and workforce-versus-alternatives comparison.
- Added editable pricing, traction, validation, ask, and milestone placeholders.
- Added current/prototype/roadmap labels, trust architecture, validation plan, and three-stage roadmap.
- Added CleanDhaka workflow bottleneck, intervention, decision point, operational value, and pilot metrics.

## Founder inputs still required

- Verified target market data and source.
- Current traction and named/approved pilot status.
- Customer-interview count and validated findings.
- Tested pricing assumptions and pricing logic.
- Verified time-saved, resolution, retention, and willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Revenue status.
- Exact funding, pilot, or partnership ask.
- Three dated milestones with accountable owners.
